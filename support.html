Restoring Lifetime Studio on a new iPhone or iPad

Open Projects in Crossloom, scroll to the bottom, and tap Restore Purchases (or open Studio Pass and tap Restore purchase). Make sure your device is signed in with the same Apple Account — or belongs to the same Apple Family Sharing group — that completed the one-time Crossloom Lifetime Studio purchase.

How do I import a multi-page PDF or OXS pattern?

  1. On Projects, tap Import PDF or OXS, then tap File in the top bar (or share any .pdf / .oxs file directly to Crossloom from the Files app).
  2. Keep Exclude P.01 cover sheet when detected checked so non-grid cover pages stay out of your stitch grid automatically (coverless multi-page charts keep page 1 automatically).
  3. For digital vector PDFs (up to 64 symbol colors and 500×500 stitches), Crossloom reconstructs 2D coordinates, pairs explicit legend DMC codes, and deduplicates horizontal and vertical overlap seams. For scanned or flattened PDFs (up to 6 pages), Crossloom converts the page artwork into a new stitch grid at 38 Quick, 54 Classic, or 76 Detailed columns. Full-stitch .oxs XML files import directly with thread brand codes preserved.
  4. Preview the dimensions and top thread counts for free, then tap Open in Hoop to save and start stitching.

How do I convert a photo into a stitch or needlepoint chart?

  1. On Projects, tap Photo to Pattern, then tap + Photo to pick an image from your library.
  2. Drag the split-curtain handle left and right to compare your photograph 1:1 against the woven stitches.
  3. Adjust Grid Density (38 / 54 / 76 columns), Fabric Mesh (14-ct Aida, 16-ct Aida, or 18-mesh Belt), and Thread Palette (8 / 14 / 20 colors matched from 454 standard cotton color references). Tap any spool chip to isolate where that color stitches.

Switching between A4 and US Letter Printable PDFs

Tap the printer icon on Projects or inside the stitch workspace to open Printable Pattern Sheet. Use the top segmented toggle to switch live between A4 (210×297 mm) and US Letter (8.5×11 in) vector symbol sheets before saving or printing.

Using Color Spotlight, Row Guide & Unpick

  • Color Spotlight: Tap any bobbin chip along the bottom dock (or iPad/landscape sidebar) to highlight that single thread across the canvas. While Spotlight is active in Stitch mode, other thread colors are protected from accidental over-painting.
  • Row Guide: Use the brass ‹ R12 › stepper to move your horizontal row marker up or down one row at a time (Night Linen darkroom mode applies to the stitch workspace and stash sheet).
  • Unpick & Undo: Switch the tool mode to Unpick to remove completed stitches, or tap the top Undo arrow to revert up to 60 recent mark or unpick strokes.

How do I reset stitch progress or delete a custom project?

On Projects, press and hold any row under Pattern library to open its menu (Open in Hoop, Reset Progress to 0%, or Delete Project) and confirm in the prompt. Inside the stitch workspace, tap the project name pill in the top-left corner and choose Reset Stitch Progress to 0%.

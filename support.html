Restoring Lifetime Studio on a new iPhone or iPad

Open Projects in Crossloom, scroll to the bottom, and tap Restore Purchases (or open Studio Pass and tap Restore purchase). Make sure your device is signed in with the same Apple Account — or belongs to the same Apple Family Sharing group — that completed the one-time Crossloom Lifetime Studio purchase.

How do I import a multi-page PDF or OXS pattern?

  1. On Projects, tap Import PDF or OXS, then tap File in the top bar (or share any .pdf / .oxs file directly to Crossloom from the Files app).
  2. Keep Exclude P.01 cover sheet checked if page 1 of your PDF is a cover photo or floss key table so legend text stays out of your stitch grid.
  3. For digital vector PDFs, Crossloom automatically reconstructs 2D coordinates and deduplicates horizontal and vertical overlap seams. For scanned or flattened PDFs, Crossloom automatically converts the pages into a stitch grid and lets you choose 38 Quick, 54 Classic, or 76 Detailed resolution.
  4. Review the verified stitch dimensions and floss yardage for free, then tap Open in Hoop to start stitching.

How do I convert a photo into a stitch or needlepoint chart?

  1. On Projects, tap Photo to DMC Pattern, then tap + Photo to pick an image from your library.
  2. Drag the split-curtain handle left and right to compare your photograph against the woven stitches.
  3. Adjust Grid Density (38 / 54 / 76 columns), Fabric Mesh (14-ct Aida, 16-ct Linen, or 18-mesh Belt), and DMC Palette (8 / 14 / 20 colors). Tap any spool chip to isolate where that thread stitches.

Using Color Spotlight, Row Guide & Unpick

  • Color Spotlight: Tap any bobbin chip along the bottom dock (or iPad sidebar) to highlight that single thread across the canvas. While Spotlight is active in Stitch mode, other thread colors are protected from accidental over-painting. Tap All or tap the selected bobbin again to clear Spotlight.
  • Row Guide: Use the brass ‹ R12 › stepper to move your horizontal row marker up or down one row at a time.
  • Unpick & Undo: Switch the tool mode to Unpick to remove completed stitches, or tap the top Undo arrow to revert your last mark or unpick stroke.

How do I reset stitch progress or delete a custom project?

On Projects, press and hold any row under Pattern library to open its menu (Open in Hoop, Reset Progress to 0%, or Delete Project). Inside the stitch workspace, you can also tap the project name pill in the top-left corner and choose Reset Stitch Progress to 0%.

Where are my charts and photos stored?

Exclusively on your device. Crossloom operates 100% offline and never uploads your files anywhere. Back up exported printable PDFs to iCloud Drive or Files if you plan to erase your device.

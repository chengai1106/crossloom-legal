Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

Short version: Crossloom operates zero developer servers, zero analytics, zero advertising, and zero third-party SDKs. All pattern parsing and photo conversion run locally on your iPhone or iPad.

1. On-Device Processing, Local Storage & System Backups

All PDF charts, OXS XML patterns, selected photographs, stitch progress records, and thread stash checklists are processed locally on your device. Saved custom projects and stitch progress are stored inside Crossloom's app container (Application Support), which may be included in your own encrypted iTunes/Finder or iCloud Device Backups according to your iOS / iPadOS system settings. Temporary import and PDF export working files are stored in temporary app storage and cleaned automatically. Deleting Crossloom removes its local container from your device.

2. Photos, Files & User-Initiated Sharing

When you convert a photograph in Photo to Pattern, Crossloom uses Apple's out-of-process PhotosPicker. Only the specific image you choose is delivered to Crossloom for in-memory downsampling and color matching; Crossloom cannot browse the rest of your photo library, and your original photograph is never written to persistent storage (saving a project stores only the quantized stitch grid and thread codes).

When you open a .pdf or .oxs chart via the system Files picker or Open in Crossloom, Crossloom reads only the file you selected using coordinated security-scoped access. Generated printable A4 / US Letter PDFs leave the app only when you explicitly pick a destination in the system Share Sheet.

3. Tracking & Apple Privacy Manifest

Crossloom does not track you across apps or websites. The bundled Apple Privacy Manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy) declares NSPrivacyTracking = false, NSPrivacyCollectedDataTypes = [], and NSPrivacyAccessedAPITypes = [].

4. In-App Purchases

The optional one-time Crossloom Lifetime Studio unlock (com.cheng.crossloom.lifetime) and purchase restoration are processed directly by Apple via StoreKit 2. Crossloom never receives or stores your payment card details. Apple's privacy policy governs App Store transactions.

5. Optional Customer Support Emails

If you choose to email customer support at xiangmucheng001@gmail.com (or if Apple App Review contacts our dedicated review address), we receive your email address and any diagnostic details you voluntarily include solely to reply to your request. Support emails are never sold, used for marketing, or shared with third parties, and you may request deletion of prior support correspondence at any time.

6. Children's Privacy

Crossloom is rated 4+, collects no personal data inside the app from anyone including children, and contains no social feeds, chat, or advertising.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Short version: Crossloom does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There is no user account, no cloud upload, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDK of any kind.

1. 100% On-Device Processing & Storage

All PDF charts, OXS XML patterns, selected photographs, stitch progress records, thread stash checklists, and generated printable PDFs are processed and stored locally inside Crossloom's private sandbox container on your iPhone or iPad. Crossloom never uploads your patterns or photos to any external server. Deleting the app removes this local data from your device.

2. Photos & Files Selection

When you convert a photograph in Photo to Pattern, Crossloom uses Apple's system PhotosPicker. Only the specific image you choose is delivered to Crossloom for on-device color matching; Crossloom cannot browse or access the rest of your photo library.

When you open a .pdf or .oxs chart via the system Files picker or Open in Crossloom, Crossloom reads only the file you selected and stores a local working copy inside its on-device container. Generated printable pattern sheets leave your device only when you explicitly choose a destination in the iOS / iPadOS Share Sheet.

3. Tracking & Apple Privacy Manifest

Crossloom does not track you across apps or websites. The app's bundled Apple Privacy Manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy) declares NSPrivacyTracking = false, an empty list of collected data types (NSPrivacyCollectedDataTypes = []), and only standard on-device preferences storage (NSPrivacyAccessedAPICategoryUserDefaults under Apple reason CA92.1).

4. In-App Purchases

The optional one-time Crossloom Lifetime Studio unlock (com.cheng.crossloom.lifetime) and purchase restoration are processed directly by Apple via StoreKit 2. Crossloom never receives or stores your billing or payment card information. Apple's privacy policy governs App Store transactions.

5. Children's Privacy

Crossloom is rated 4+, collects zero personal information from anyone including children, and contains no social feeds, external web views, chat, or advertising.

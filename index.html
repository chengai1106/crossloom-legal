Crossloom

Calm, offline cross-stitch & needlepoint studio for iPad and iPhone

Universal iOS & iPadOS 17.0+ · 100% On-Device · No Subscription

Crossloom joins multi-page digital PDF and OXS charts into one continuous stitch grid, converts personal photos into cotton-matched cross-stitch or 18-mesh needlepoint patterns, tracks every stitch offline, and exports crisp vector A4 / US Letter printable sheets.

Core Studio Tools

  • Multi-page PDF & OXS Light-Table — Automatic overlap seam deduplication for vector symbol PDFs, OXS XML support, cover-sheet exclusion, and automatic grid conversion for scanned PDFs.
  • Photo to Pattern Darkroom — Perceptual CIELAB matching across all 454 standard solid stranded cotton shades, live split-curtain preview, and 14-ct Aida, 16-ct Linen, or 18-mesh Needlepoint Belt proportions.
  • Distraction-Free Stitch Hoop — Single-color Spotlight with over-paint protection, 4× optical loupe, bidirectional brass row guide, drag-to-mark and unpick with stroke undo, and Night Linen dark mode.
  • Floss Stash & Vector Sheets — Live 8m skein and fabric cut calculator (+3" framing border) plus multi-page printable vector PDF symbol charts.

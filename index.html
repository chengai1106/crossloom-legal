Crossloom

Calm, offline cross-stitch & needlepoint studio for iPad and iPhone

Universal iOS & iPadOS 17.0+ · On-Device Processing · No Subscription

Crossloom joins multi-page digital PDF and OXS charts into one continuous stitch grid, converts personal photos into up to 20 cotton-matched shades across 454 standard thread color references, tracks every stitch offline, and exports crisp vector A4 & US Letter printable sheets.

Core Studio Tools

  • Multi-page PDF & OXS Light-Table — Overlap seam deduplication for vector symbol PDFs, full-stitch OXS XML import, automatic cover-sheet detection, and image-to-grid pattern conversion for scanned PDFs (up to 6 pages).
  • Photo to Pattern Darkroom — Perceptual CIELAB color matching (8, 14, or 20 colors selected from 454 standard stranded cotton color references compatible with DMC numbering), live 1:1 split-curtain preview, and 14-ct Aida, 16-ct Aida, or 18-mesh Needlepoint Belt proportions.
  • Distraction-Free Stitch Hoop — Single-color Spotlight with over-paint protection, dynamic optical stitch loupe, bidirectional brass row guide, gap-free drag-to-mark and unpick with 60-step stroke undo, VoiceOver cell navigation, and Night Linen darkroom mode.
  • Floss Stash & Vector Sheets — Live 8m skein and fabric cut calculator (+3" framing border) plus multi-page printable A4 and US Letter vector PDF symbol charts (up to 64 collision-free symbols).
